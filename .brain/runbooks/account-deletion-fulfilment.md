# Account-deletion fulfilment working procedure

After access revocation, verified store notifications for known personal purchase chains can still update retained billing evidence. The audit event marks reconciliation after revocation. Inspect those events and subscription status during processing; neither notification delivery nor a suspended state proves that the requester cancelled renewal in the correct store account. An unlinked first notification remains rejected for manual reconciliation. Retention and final erasure treatment of store evidence remain undecided.

The Mobile app access screen also offers platform-specific official cancellation instructions when its native store Manage action is unavailable. This keeps a guidance path visible before account revocation, but the operator must not infer that a requester opened it or cancelled in the correct store account.

The public Delete account page and email-verification review include official Apple and Google Play cancellation help for a requester who cannot sign in. These links do not prove the person used the correct store account or completed cancellation. The operator must keep store cancellation separate from access revocation and erasure processing.

Status: local implementation and operator review only; not a completed production process (28 September 2026).

The operator dashboard now has 82 selected read-only linked-record counts for requests after access revocation: one for each of the 74 directly named Prisma `userId`/`*UserId` fields in the current source scan, seven member-linked counts, and one personal mobile store provider-event count linked through its purchase chain. The member-linked counts cover documents, support cases, contributions, payment receipts, financing requests, financing facilities and repayments. This catches records linked to the member even when a different staff account created or reviewed them, and flags store event payloads for separate retention review without returning their contents. Use the counts to identify categories needing review, then inspect the wider sources below. Counts can overlap on one record; free text, JSON, other indirect links and shared-record contents are not inspected. Zero counts are never a completion check: other shared finance links, content, external processors and backups remain outside this query. The [source-field coverage test](../../packages/db/src/queries/privacy-processing-inventory-coverage.test.ts) and [operator-rendering test](../../apps/dashboard/src/components/privacy-processing-count-list.test.tsx) passed on 28 September; they verify selected coverage and display, not erasure.
After review or a processor contact, record the category, action/reference and result as restricted processing evidence in the operator dashboard. Do not enter credentials or identity-document numbers. The note is retained with the request and audited by category; it is an operator assertion, not proof that a processor complied. Verify the referenced result separately and keep the request `processing` until the completion gate below is satisfied.

## Request and access sequence

The signed-in mobile deletion screen links to Mobile app access so the requester can use its store Manage control before revocation. The operator should still confirm that cancellation is user-managed and separate from the deletion workflow; navigation to billing does not prove cancellation or stop renewal charges.

1. An authenticated account holder or the email-verified public route creates an `AccountPrivacyRequest` linked to a support case. The public email link proves control of that address; it does not complete identity and scope verification.
2. A current platform owner reviews the request and records identity/scope evidence. Do not put passwords, document numbers or copies of identity documents in the evidence field.
3. Before access revocation, load the exact current membership scope in the operator form and acknowledge each cooperative and role. Confirm replacement administration for every cooperative where the user has an admin role and replacement platform ownership where applicable. Record the handover and the need to review retained records. The transaction rejects a changed membership set or role and the last administrator/owner; refresh and review again after a scope change.
4. Use the distinct `revokeAccess` action. It removes login credentials, mobile sessions, push registrations and auth challenges, anonymises account identifiers and marks the user deleted. Database-backed session lookups reject the deleted user, including previously signed web cookies. The privacy request stays `processing`; the support case and audit log record only access revocation. The original email is held in restricted processing evidence for later completion contact.

## Processing inventory and decisions still required

For each category, record the lawful/contractual retention basis, exact retention period or review event, eligible fields to erase or de-identify, the system/processor owner, execution evidence and result. Do not infer that every row linked to a deleted user can be deleted: cooperative and audit records may be shared or financially material.

## Completion gate

There is no exposed `completed` action yet. Add one only after the above categories have approved decisions and tested execution paths. Before closing a request, reconcile processor outcomes and backup limitations, deliver a truthful completion notice to the held contact address, record what was deleted and what remains restricted with its reason, and apply the approved rule to the temporary contact and evidence. Do not mark a request complete merely because access was revoked or a notification was queued.

Production execution also waits for the coordinated Neon credential rotation, isolated hosting and end-to-end privacy-route/device tests documented in [the launch task](../tasks/2026-09-24-launch-readiness.md).
