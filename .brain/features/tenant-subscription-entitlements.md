## Product-scoped native purchase errors — 28 September 2026

The installed `expo-iap` 5.6.3 purchase-error shape can include `productId` or `productIds`, but both are optional. The native listener previously treated every error as the outcome of the pending checkout; an unrelated store error could cancel its callback watchdog and unlock another purchase before the original charge was resolved. It now clears the pending checkout only when an error identifies the expected product. Unrelated or unidentified errors leave the 30-second wait and Restore-first review gate intact; a directly rejected `requestPurchase` still uses its existing catch/recovery path. ## Google RTDN envelope validation — 28 September 2026

The authenticated push handler now requires the documented top-level `version: "1.0"`, nested version on test/subscription/pending-refund events, and a positive integral subscription notification type. The handler still re-queries current Play state rather than trusting the notification type to grant access. ## Google acknowledgment after applied entitlement — 28 September 2026

The API client-verification and Google RTDN paths now acknowledge a Play purchase only after `applyVerifiedStoreEntitlement` returns `applied: true`. A verified but unapplied client result remains available for Restore/support without a false plan-change claim. An RTDN with pending acknowledgment and `applied: false` returns 503 rather than acknowledging or silently accepting the event. This follows [Google's purchase-security guidance](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/security) to acknowledge after granting entitlement. It makes timely retry, operator reconciliation and dead-letter alerts essential before live sales; source tests do not prove a signed Play purchase or redelivery.

The native listener never calls Expo IAP `finishTransaction` for Google: in installed `expo-iap` 5.6.3 that call invokes Play acknowledgment, which would bypass the server's grant-first check. It finishes an Apple transaction only when the server reports `applied: true`; `applied: false` retains the Restore/support review gate. Signed-store tests must check both callbacks and Play acknowledgment state.
An unapplied callback is also no longer recorded in the native event guard's ten-second recent-success window. A repeated store callback or explicit Restore can re-verify immediately, while the in-flight guard still deduplicates concurrent work and the review gate blocks another charge.
The native listener now ignores any purchase-error callback while an earlier purchase callback remains in server verification. The error cannot clear busy state and expose a second checkout during unresolved payment. A cancellation outside that window still releases the UI normally. Focused guard tests cover the callback-order policy; installed-store callback timing remains unverified.

## Product boundary

- A subscription belongs to a cooperative tenant. It is Halaalvest SaaS billing, not a member commitment, cooperative contribution, share position, financing obligation, repayment, or ledger balance.
- The governed plans are Free Beta, Starter, Standard, Growth, and Enterprise.
- Commercial categories remain explicit: monthly subscription fee, one-time setup fee, separately quoted add-ons, and pass-through SMS/WhatsApp/KYC/payment-provider usage.
- Core cooperative finance authority is never sold as an entitlement. A plan may narrow access or capacity, but no entitlement bypasses role, tenant, Operation Profile, record-state, readiness, finance, audit, or idempotency guards.

## Plan and entitlement contract

- Free Beta: 100 active members; admin-first pilot; time-bound access.
- Starter: 250 active members; admin-first operation with optional member login, custom domain, and WhatsApp setup.
- Standard: 1,000 active members; member login/statements, finance/operations roles, full reporting/audit, and custom domain.
- Growth: 3,000 active members; advanced roles/reporting/audit/notifications with optional white label and dedicated support.
- Enterprise: contracted capacity and custom entitlements, integrations, support, and branding.
- Effective entitlements cover member login, statements, notification depth, custom domain, white-label eligibility, role depth, report depth, and audit depth.

## Lifecycle and enforcement

- Durable statuses are `free_beta`, `trialing`, `active`, `past_due`, `grace`, `cancelled`, `suspended`, `manual`, and `enterprise`.
- The first Free Beta period begins when the tenant subscription is provisioned, not at historical tenant creation. This prevents an older tenant from receiving an already-expired first subscription.
- Expired beta/trial, past-due grace, cancellation-at-period-end, and suspension are reconciled server-side with audit evidence.
- Grace preserves existing access according to the policy snapshot but blocks expansion such as new member activation when required.
- Member login is checked in the authoritative account-access path. Active-member capacity is checked in authoritative member creation/reactivation paths. Client visibility is explanatory only.
- If subscription infrastructure is unavailable, existing core administration remains readable while member login, plan expansion, and other unsafe entitlement decisions fail closed.

## Persistence and API

- `SubscriptionPlan` stores the versioned commercial and entitlement definition.
- `TenantSubscription` is the tenant-owned current lifecycle record; add-ons, checkout intents, and invoices hang from it.
- Checkout idempotency is unique within the tenant and cannot be reused for another requested plan.
- Provider events are unique by provider/event identity and reject a duplicate identity with conflicting payload evidence.
- `subscriptions.snapshot` returns the single tenant-scoped plan/status/period/usage/limit/add-on/invoice/available-plan/source-state read model.
- `subscriptions.requestPlanChange` records a provider-neutral intent. It does not claim checkout success when no provider URL exists.
