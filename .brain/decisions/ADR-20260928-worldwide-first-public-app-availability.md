# ADR: Worldwide target for the first public mobile release

Date: 28 September 2026
Status: Accepted as the owner's distribution target; activation gated

## Decision

Prepare HalaalVest's first public Apple App Store and Google Play app release for worldwide availability, as the owner selected on 28 September. The free app download setting is separate from subscriptions and from the countries where cooperative financial features or paid software plans can be offered.

## Consequences

- Verify country-specific legal and financial-service eligibility, tax, payment and support claims, store declarations, and localized subscription prices before saving worldwide availability or enabling sales.
- Preserve the existing gate on new Production store offers until exact products, policies, signed-device transactions and provider-account requirements pass review.
- The coordinating Apple-console task saved and reload-verified app-specific availability for all 175 Apple countries or regions as `Available on App Release`; this is still a draft release setting. The owner subsequently signed the Paid Apps Agreement, which now shows `Pending User Info` until bank and U.S. tax information is supplied. Distribution-rights confirmation remains open.
- Keep the owner hold on cloud builds, deployments, OTA publication and submissions until the reported monthly reset and explicit lift.
