HalaalVest helps cooperative members and authorised staff work with their cooperative's records in one mobile app.

Each cooperative sets its own membership rules, charges, approval decisions and available funds. An eligibility estimate is not financing approval or a promise of funds. Review the cooperative's terms and all applicable costs before making a financial commitment.

The app provides account privacy requests and links to platform legal and support information. Some functions require an internet connection or an active cooperative account. Optional notifications depend on device permission.

This copy describes implemented product surfaces; it does not claim that HalaalVest or the platform operator holds deposits, lends directly, guarantees returns, has regulatory approval, or has independent Shariah certification.
