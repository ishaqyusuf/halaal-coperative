# Halaalvest Cooperative Platform

This glossary defines the domain language used for Halaalvest cooperative operations and member access. It intentionally keeps software access charges separate from cooperative finance balances and obligations.

## Member App Access

**Member App Access**:
Access by a cooperative member to the shared Halaalvest mobile app. It is distinct from a cooperative-branded white-label app.
_Avoid_: App feature, mobile subscription

**Tenant App Entitlement**:
The cooperative-level commercial entitlement that permits its members to use Member App Access. Member payment cannot override an inactive Tenant App Entitlement.
_Avoid_: Cooperative app switch, master subscription

**Member Access Coverage**:
A time-bounded record showing that one member's Member App Access fee is fully funded for a coverage term through member payment, cooperative funding, or both.
_Avoid_: Member subscription, app levy

**Member-funded Coverage**:
Member Access Coverage for which the member pays the full listed fee.
_Avoid_: Self subscription

**Cooperative-funded Coverage**:
Member Access Coverage for which the cooperative pays the full listed fee as an authorized operating expense.
_Avoid_: Free access, member benefit payment

**Subsidized Coverage**:
Member Access Coverage for which the cooperative pays a configured percentage of the listed fee and the member pays the balance.
_Avoid_: Discounted subscription, shared levy

**Coverage Term**:
The six-month or annual period for which a member's Member App Access is funded.
_Avoid_: Billing cycle, membership period
