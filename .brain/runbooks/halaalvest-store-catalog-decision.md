**Implementation constraint for the trial answer:** the current Android offer loader selects only a one-phase `P1M` recurring price for the configured base plan; it does not select or display a multi-phase introductory/free-trial offer. The iOS loader checks a one-month subscription and displays its standard `displayPrice`, without presenting introductory-offer eligibility or terms in the app. Thus a trial choice is a request for additional client, catalog and signed-device work, not a console-only setting. The current Android purchase path uses a deferred replacement for a plan change; Apple's subscription-group levels determine its upgrade/downgrade behavior. Confirm those transition rules before assigning Apple levels or promising immediate upgrades in copy.

**Store-price configuration:** Apple subscription prices are selected from storefront-specific price points; its console can derive comparable prices for other regions from a selected starting storefront, and Apple says auto-renewable subscription retail prices are not automatically adjusted for tax or foreign-exchange changes. Google configures an auto-renewing base plan with regional prices and separately configurable offers. A marketing range cannot be entered as the consumer price, and the exact requested NGN amount must be checked against each console's available price points and regional availability before confirming it. The app displays the price returned by the user's store account, which may use a non-Nigerian storefront. Sources: [Apple subscription pricing](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/manage-pricing-for-auto-renewable-subscriptions), [Apple storefront availability](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-availability-for-an-auto-renewable-subscription), [Apple subscription levels](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/in-app-purchases-and-subscriptions/auto-renewable-subscription-information), [Google subscription setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/140504?hl=en), [Google subscription lifecycle](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/subscriptions).
