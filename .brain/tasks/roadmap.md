The owner lifted the first HalaalVest mobile store release's blanket cloud-action hold on 6 October 2026; individual legal, backend, signed-build, subscription and store gates still control the next steps. The 19-path release-source gate and authenticated local Android/iOS archive-stage inspections include the personal reviewer-grant query and generated migration; both inspected archives matched primary `main`. The local public-page preflight now checks the deletion verification route with and without a synthetic challenge, but approved publication and delivered-link redemption remain open. The next source checkpoint is to repeat archive inspection after changes and bind the first permitted build to its server-accepted upload and signed artifact. Then complete isolated backend/schema readiness, Apple and Google individual subscription catalogs at the approved Nigerian prices, owner review of Apple's equal six-month/annual prices in 152 of 175 saved territories, and a decision on the unsaved approximately 2:1 overseas alternative whose preview has 29 outliers above 2.5:1. No repricing or paid-sale territory should be saved before that choice and any required outlier corrections. Before Play publisher conversion, resolve ownership of all 17 apps in the existing Personal account; if unrelated apps exist, assess a separate eligible Zeroes Organization account and transfers. Matching D-U-N-S/document/contact verification and the chosen Organization publishing path remain required before HalaalVest app creation. Synthetic paid-feature and unpaid reviewer accounts and signed-device purchase/Restore/renewal/refund and repeat-review proof follow. Public legal approval/publication, deletion completion, staffed reconciliation and dependency/credential gates remain open before public sales. Local archive inspection is not store acceptance.
